New Here?

We would love to welcome you this Sunday. Here is everything you need to know before you come.

Your First Visit

What to Expect

Our Sunday service lasts about an hour and a half. We sing together, pray together, and hear a message from the Bible that is clear and practical. You will not be singled out or asked to give. Come as you are.

When to arrive

Doors open 20 minutes before the service. Arriving early gives you time to find a seat and meet a few people.

What to wear

Whatever is comfortable. Most people dress casually or smart casual.

Kids

Children are welcome in the service, and we have Bible lessons and activities for kids during the message. Ask any of our volunteers to show you where.

Language

Our services are in a mix of English and Filipino, so everyone can follow along.
