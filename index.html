Welcome

We are glad you are here.

Christ Community of Grace is a Bible-believing, gospel-centered church in Calamba, Philippines. We gather to worship God, grow in His Word, and serve one another and our community with the love of Christ. Whether you are a long-time believer or simply curious about faith, you are welcome here. Our doors are open to anyone seeking truth, community, and grace.
